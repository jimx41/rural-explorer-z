Privacy Policy

Rural Explorer Z

Last updated: 6 September 2026

The short version: Rural Explorer Z does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Your progress is saved on your own device and never leaves it.

Who we are

Rural Explorer Z ("the game") is developed and published by Z Sphere ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy, contact us at itsjimlive@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect personal information. Specifically, the game does not ask for or receive:

  • your name, email address, phone number, or any account details;
  • your location;
  • your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone;
  • your device identifiers, advertising ID, or IP address;
  • analytics, crash reports, or usage statistics.

The game has no login, no server of our own, and no means of sending your information anywhere.

Information stored on your device

So that the game remembers you between visits, it saves a small amount of progress data in your browser's local storage, under the key keepers-turn-v1. This contains only:

  • how many walks you have taken;
  • your best completion time;
  • how many walks you have finished;
  • how many foxes you have greeted;
  • how many stars you have found in total;
  • which hat you last chose and which cosmetics you have unlocked.

This data stays on your device. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else, and it contains nothing that could identify you. You can erase it at any time by clearing your browser's site data, or, on Android, by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Photographs you take in game

The game includes a snapshot feature that saves a picture of your current view. The image is produced on your device and saved directly to your device's downloads. It is never uploaded to us or to any third party.

Third-party services

The game loads its 3D graphics library, three.js, from the public Cloudflare CDN at cdnjs.cloudflare.com. As with any request to a website, Cloudflare may automatically receive your IP address and basic technical details of the request as part of delivering that file. We do not receive this information, and we do not control it. You can read Cloudflare's privacy policy at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

The game uses no other third-party service. There is no advertising network, no analytics provider, and no social media integration.

Cookies

The game does not use cookies.

Children's privacy

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because we collect nothing, we do not knowingly hold any child's data. If you believe a child has somehow provided us with personal information, contact us and we will look into it.

Your rights

Data protection laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, and delete personal data held about you. Because we hold no personal data about you at all, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, or delete. The progress data described above is entirely under your control on your own device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the revised version on this page and update the date above. If a future version of the game ever collects data, we will say so here plainly before it does.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to itsjimlive@gmail.com.
